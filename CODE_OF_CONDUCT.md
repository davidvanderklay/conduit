# Code of conduct

Conduit welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. This applies to repository issues, pull requests, Discussions,
and other project spaces.

Treat people with respect. Keep criticism about the work, explain disagreements,
and make room for people learning the codebase. Respect people's privacy and
their decision to stop a conversation.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate
disruption, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable.
Do not post credentials or other users' account, profile, or watch-history data.

## Reporting and moderation

Report abusive public content through GitHub's reporting tools. To request
private contact with the maintainer, open an issue titled `Community contact
request` without personal or incident details. The maintainer will arrange a
private channel. Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may remove content, close or lock discussions, warn participants,
or restrict participation depending on the conduct and whether it repeats.
Reports will be handled with care for the privacy of the people involved.
